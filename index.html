About

Designer at heart,
product thinker by craft.

Hi, I'm Long. I'm a Sr. UX/UI Designer in Houston, currently at NFP, An Aon Company, and moving into product management. For nearly thirty years I've taken messy, complicated problems and turned them into experiences that feel obvious.

I lead with research, move fast with prototypes, and stay close to the build until every detail earns its place. My background runs through software, medical, and consulting, which taught me to speak fluent stakeholder and fluent engineer, and to translate between the two.

I use AI as a creative partner across the craft: to synthesize research faster, explore more directions, pressure test ideas, and clear away repetitive work. It makes me a sharper designer and a stronger product manager, multiplying judgment instead of replacing it.

These days I'm deep in product: pairing design instinct with roadmap thinking, and learning what it takes to ship outcomes, not just screens.
